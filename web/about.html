About KidNews Earth

What this is

Every day, KidNews Earth picks one article from NASA Earth Observatory and turns it into a short story for middle-school readers (grades 6 to 8), with a quiz to check what you learned.

How stories are made

An AI model writes the summary, the story and the quiz from the original article. A person then reads every story and approves it before it appears here. Each story links to the article it is based on, so you can read the original too.

NASA did not write, review or endorse these stories. If something looks wrong, trust the original article.

Your privacy

This site is designed to collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no comments, no ads and no tracking cookies. Quiz answers stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

Images and credits

NASA content is generally free to use for education with NASA credited as the source. Each image shows its own credit line.

Stories are written by AI from NASA Earth Observatory articles and checked by a person before they go up. Not reviewed or endorsed by NASA.
